# Medical Device Sales & Service Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes medical device sales and after-sales service data to identify patterns in revenue performance, product demand, hospital purchasing, service operations, downtime, warranty, and customer satisfaction.

The project combines **SQL, Python, and Power BI** to transform raw healthcare business data into meaningful insights that can support sales monitoring, service management, and operational decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Medical device companies generate large amounts of sales and service data across different devices, hospitals, regions, and service activities. Analyzing these datasets together can help identify high-performing products, understand customer purchasing patterns, monitor service performance, and identify operational areas requiring attention.

This project focuses on answering questions such as:

* Which medical devices and categories generate the most sales?
* Which regions and hospital segments contribute the most revenue?
* How does sales performance change over time?
* Which devices generate more service requirements?
* How do downtime and resolution time vary across service cases?
* How does warranty status affect service activity and cost?
* What patterns can be observed in customer satisfaction?

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall medical device sales performance.
* Identify high-performing devices and device categories.
* Compare sales across regions and hospital segments.
* Analyze monthly sales trends.
* Evaluate after-sales service activity.
* Analyze device downtime and resolution time.
* Compare warranty and non-warranty service cases.
* Examine service costs and customer satisfaction.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for business reporting.

## 💡 Key Insights

* Regional sales performance showed considerable variation, with certain regions contributing a larger share of total revenue.
* A relatively small group of medical devices accounted for a significant portion of overall sales.
* Device categories differed substantially in revenue contribution and demand.
* Hospital type and customer segment influenced medical device purchasing patterns.
* Monthly sales showed variation over time rather than remaining completely consistent.
* Service requests and service requirements were concentrated around specific device categories and models.
* Downtime, resolution time, and service costs varied across different service cases and warranty statuses.
* Combining sales and service data provides a broader view of revenue performance, product reliability, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
